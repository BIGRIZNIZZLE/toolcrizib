    Open in Chrome

    Adding to the Home Screen works in Safari. If you opened this from a text, Discord, Instagram or another app, use the Open in Safari button above, or tap ••• / Share → Open in Safari, or paste the link into Safari.

    Use Chrome. In another app, tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome (or Open in browser).

  2. 2

    Add it to your Home Screen

    Install the app

    Tap Open Tool Crizib below. Then in Safari tap Share (the square with the arrow; if you don’t see it, tap ••• first), choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

    Tap Install Tool Crizib above if you see it. Otherwise open Tool Crizib, tap Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen) → Install.

  3. 3

    Open Tool Crizib from your Home Screen

    Find the Tool Crizib icon (the steel drill) and open it from there. It runs full screen and works offline.

  4. 4

    Create your account with email

    In Tool Crizib, open the Settings tab. In ☁️ Account & sync, type your email and tap Email me a sign-in link. No password needed.

    Open the email. Your first one is titled “Confirm your email address”; after that it’s “Your sign-in link”. Press and hold the link (Confirm email address or Sign in), tap Copy Link, switch back to Tool Crizib, paste it into Sign-in link or code, and tap Sign in.

    Don’t tap the link. On iPhone it opens Safari and signs in there, not in your Home Screen app.

    An account is optional. It only keeps your phone and other devices in sync.
